About Peng Salon & SPA

A Legacy of Luxury, Innovation & Craftsmanship

Established in 2004 on Khayaban-e-Ittehad, Phase 2 DHA Karachi, Peng Salon & SPA has set the benchmark for high-end beauty, therapeutic spa experiences, and bespoke hair design in Pakistan.

Beyond offering world-class salon experiences for both gentlemen and ladies, Peng expanded into the Peng Beauty Academy to train the next generation of salon artisans, and Atelier By Peng for high-fashion runway and bridal creations.
