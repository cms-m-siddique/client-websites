Designer Story

Crafting Modern Heirlooms at Heavenly Regalia - HR

At Heavenly Regalia - HR, lead designer Nazneen Tariq combines age-old silver artistry with contemporary luxury aesthetics. Operating out of D.H.A Phase 6, Karachi, the studio specializes in fine silver jewelry set with rare precious and semi-precious gemstones.

From raw diamond slice statement pieces and lustrous pearl strands to brilliant rhodium of white gold polish, every design reflects majestic artistry and royal grandeur.

Frequently Asked Questions

We specialize in 925 sterling silver embellished with natural precious & semi-precious gemstones, raw diamond slices, and South Sea pearls, finished with durable rhodium of white gold polish.
You can schedule a private consultation at our DHA Phase 6 studio or via WhatsApp. Nazneen Tariq reviews your outfit, gem preferences, and creates bespoke sketches before crafting your piece.
Our atelier is situated in D.H.A Phase 6, Karachi, Pakistan. Studio visits are available by appointment.
Rhodium is a rare precious metal in the platinum family. Electroplating silver with rhodium white gold gives the silver the identical brilliant mirror finish and scratch resistance of solid white gold.
