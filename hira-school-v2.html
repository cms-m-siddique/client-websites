About Hira Public School

Over 25 Years Of Quality Education & Character Building

Founded in 1992 under the leadership of Principal Khurshid Ahmed, Hira Public School was established to serve the Muslim community in Manzoor Colony, Karachi. Today, with over 2,500 students, we stand as a beacon of academic excellence and Islamic moral values.

Our overarching mission is to prepare students for success in both this world and the hereafter. We achieve this by blending rigorous scientific study with strong ethical instruction, state-of-the-art chemical laboratories, and rich co-curricular opportunities.

Information Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding admissions, board curriculum, science labs, and school life.

Hira Public School follows the Karachi Board of Secondary Education curriculum. We prepare secondary students rigorously for Board Examinations in Science and General groups, with a stellar record of 26 A+ grades and 60 A grades.
Children who are 25 months (2+ years) or older are eligible for our Preschool / Playgroup section. We focus on gentle social acclimatization, basic literacy, and motor skills.
Our campus houses dedicated Chemical, Physics, and Biology laboratories equipped with modern instruments, safety protocols, and experienced lab instructors to conduct all compulsory secondary practicals.
Students from Primary and Secondary classes can submit original essays, poems, drawings, and articles in English or Urdu to the editorial board headed by senior teachers during the second term of the academic year.
The school is situated at G-7/1, Allama Iqbal Road, Manzoor Colony, Karachi. Admission office hours are Monday through Saturday, from 8:00 AM to 1:30 PM.
