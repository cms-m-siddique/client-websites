About Nisha Beauty Salon

13+ Years of Unmatched Elegance & Certified Beauty Mastery

Founded in 2013 in the heart of Bahadurabad, Nisha Beauty Salon is Karachi's trusted haven for exquisite bridal transformations, skin therapy, and luxury hair pampering.

Our lead artist, Nisha, is internationally qualified with an IMA Certification from the London College of Makeup. Every service is crafted with precision, using world-class international brands like Guinot Paris, ensuring flawless results tailored to your unique features.
